- A queued merge that the merge check refuses, and that has a source that had messages and has none left in the view (removed from the store, or hidden by its filter), is now dropped with a warning that names what the check found, and its other sources are offered again, unless two or more other sources remain and the check finds a problem among them (#321). It was refused into the merge quarantine, except where a source beside it doesn't resolve or has no children, which 0.24.1 merged. `topologyPolicy`'s documentation said "a merge that WOULD mint a crossed node is never executed: it is refused into the merge quarantine"; it now goes on: "unless one of its sources had messages and has none left in the view: then it is dropped, with a warning naming what the check found, and its other sources are offered again, except where two or more others remain and the check finds a problem among them, which is refused as before". Such a merge is still never executed. Where such an entry was refused and is now dropped, an operator no longer gets a `topology_violation` quarantine record, the operator error that comes with it, or critical health while the record stands; the warning is its only trace. An entry with no such source, or with a problem among two or more other sources, is refused into the quarantine as before.
