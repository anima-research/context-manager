- `WindowedPassthroughStrategy` writes its anchor again at the next compile or read of the anchor (`renderAsNewest`, `getAnchorOrdinal()`) when a write of it failed, until one lands; a preview (`dryRun`) doesn't. Before, a sequence anchor's conversion to an ordinal whose write failed was kept in memory and never written: once a row stored before the anchor's sequence was presented after the row it named, a reload converted the sequence again to a later ordinal, and its window left out rows the running instance had sent. The conversion's failure is logged once, saying it is written at the next read; the failure of a move's write (`setAnchor`, `setAnchorOrdinal`, a re-anchor) still reaches its caller, and the next read writes that move too.
