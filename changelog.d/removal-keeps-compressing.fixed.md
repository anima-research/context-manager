- **Hosts that remove old history, or hide it with a `viewFilter`:** removing the messages of more than half an autobiographical store's chunk records (`removeMessage`, `removeMessages`, `removeBodyGroup`, an operator's suppress), or reopening the store under a filter that hides them, no longer stops its chunk compression for good. Before, the chain-break guard counted such records as a renumbered store, halted chunk compression and logged a `FAIL-CLOSED` error, while `getCompressionDebt()` read `healthy`. Now the guard counts a record only when its store can't show it ever held any of the record's ids (through a view without `held`, as before). Where the records removals and filters emptied would still pass the threshold, it asks whether the oldest and newest of them name messages on the branch's messages chain, and halts as before when either doesn't, as in a store whose messages chain broke under chronicle before 0.5.0. A store that tripped through removals or filters chunks the messages that arrived since the trip at its next open, and its compression then works through them.
- `getCompressionDebt()` reads `critical` while the guard holds, with `error` saying what happened and `failure: 'chunk-records'`, an addition to `failure`'s union.
