- **Hosts that remove old history, or hide it with a `viewFilter`:** removing the messages of more than half an autobiographical store's chunk records (`removeMessage`, `removeMessages`, `removeBodyGroup`, an operator's suppress), or reopening the store under a filter that hides them, no longer stops its compression for good. Before, the chain-break guard counted such records as a renumbered store, halted all compression and logged a `FAIL-CLOSED` error, while `getCompressionDebt()` read `healthy`. Now the guard counts a record only when its store can't show it ever held any of the record's ids (through a view without `held`, as before), and a store that tripped this way chunks the messages that arrived since the trip at its next open, and its compression then works through them.
- `getCompressionDebt()` reads `critical` while the guard holds, with `error` saying what happened and `failure: 'chunk-records'`, an addition to `failure`'s union.
