- **After the provider refuses a summarizer request for a binding mismatch, `AutobiographicalStrategy` sends that model's later summarizer requests without carriers.** On a model that binds its thinking, on an account that enforces the binding, every compression or merge request that recalls a summary with its signed carrier was refused, then retried text-only by the carrier fallback, so each was sent twice with an error logged. Now the first such refusal (an `invalid_request` saying the block is "bound to a different conversation") teaches the strategy instance, and its later summarizer requests to that model are sent as the fallback's retry is, once, logging no error. Carriers are still captured. Any other rejection that mentions thinking keeps the one-shot fallback, and a model or account that doesn't refuse sees no change.
