- **After the provider refuses a summarizer request for a binding mismatch, `AutobiographicalStrategy` sends that model's later summarizer requests without carriers.** On a model that binds its thinking, on an account that enforces the binding, every compression or merge request that recalls a summary with its signed carrier was refused, then retried text-only by the carrier fallback, so each was sent twice with an error logged. Now the first such refusal (an `invalid_request` saying the block is "bound to a different conversation") teaches the strategy, and its later summarizer requests to that model through the same membrane are sent as the fallback's retry is, once, logging no error. A learned strip emits no `compression:carrier-transport-fallback` event, and the error line an L1 logs when it resumes from a carrier rejection known from progress now quotes "carrier rejection known from progress, or from a binding refusal of this model". Carriers are still captured. Any other rejection that mentions thinking keeps the one-shot fallback, and a model or account that doesn't refuse sees no change.
