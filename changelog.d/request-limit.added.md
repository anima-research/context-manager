While the autobiographical strategy's multiplier is provisional, it prices the request at the calibration's ceiling, so a first request after a reset fits for any true rate up to it, and its sample then replaces the provisional multiplier, or confirms it where the two are within 2%. `RequestOverhead` gains `messages`, the content of each message a request sends besides the context and its message-position injections, such as a tool result a caller adds after the compile. A limit that leaves the context no room, or a request a strategy compiles past it, is refused with an `OverBudgetError`.
