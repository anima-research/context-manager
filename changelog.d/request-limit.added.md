- `compile`'s `requestLimitTokens` option keeps the whole request within a provider's limit: the context, with any tool-result stubs the autobiographical strategy's pairing repair adds, the message-position injections, `requestOverhead` and the budget's `reserveForResponse`, in the context's units, the strategy's over-budget grace included. While the autobiographical strategy's multiplier is provisional, it prices the request at the calibration's ceiling by scaling its price at the provisional multiplier. The store rounds each block's price at that multiplier, by up to half a token, so for any true rate up to the ceiling a first request after a reset can pass the limit by up to half a token a block, scaled by the ceiling over the provisional multiplier. That request's sample, where the calibration's band accepts it, then replaces the provisional multiplier, or confirms it where the two are within 2%. `RequestOverhead` gains `messages`, the content of each message a request sends besides the context and its message-position injections, such as a tool result a caller adds after the compile. It's priced as the context prices the same blocks sent whole, and counted wherever the overhead is: against the limit, in `RenderStats.request.overheadTokens`, and in a calibration sample given it. The strategy's target and hard bound are each lowered only where the limit is below them, which `RenderStats.request` reports as `limitTokens` and `allowanceTokens`, with the stubs' price as `stubTokens`. Where the stubs take the window past the limit, the strategy selects once more with their price reserved, and refuses for the stubs only if that select can't make room. A limit that leaves the context no room, or a request a strategy compiles past it, is refused with an `OverBudgetError`. Passthrough and windowed-passthrough keep within it as a cap on their room, as the store's estimator prices the request, since they don't calibrate. A limit needs `requestOverhead`: one without it, or one that isn't a positive finite number, is refused with a `TypeError` before anything is selected. The autobiographical strategy's over-budget refusals under a limit, the no-room one included, name it in their diagnostics as `requestLimitTokens`, and, where the limit lowered the bound, what it left the context as `requestAllowanceTokens`; so does the compile's own refusal of a request past the limit, and a preview that reports a refusal carrying them copies them. A refusal the limit made, by its bound, by leaving no room or by the stubs past what it left, keeps a provisional multiplier, since a lower one can't make the context fit there. Without `requestLimitTokens`, every select is as it was.
