- Once a store's host accepts a round, the first compile after this lands strips the thinking of every retained reply, since none has a stamp yet: one prompt-cache rewrite. Replies stored after that keep their thinking until a rewrite reaches them, provided the manager can vouch for the compile each was minted under: the branch's last accepted compile, or one sent after it that sent the same. The host stores each round as it went out, and defers other writes until the turn ends (see `acceptRound`); an edit to what came before a reply, made after the reply is stored, strips its thinking.
- Once the pass is engaged, `AutobiographicalStrategy` renders and prices the live window as under `carrierPolicy: 'live-strip'`, whatever is configured: a summary's carriers were minted in the summarizer's request, so an enforcing account refuses them there. `'full'` still governs a store whose host has never accepted a round. Mint and merge requests are unchanged.
