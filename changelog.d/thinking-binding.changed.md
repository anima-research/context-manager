- Once a store's host accepts a round, the first compile after this lands strips the thinking of every retained reply, since none has a stamp yet: one prompt-cache rewrite. Replies stored after that keep their thinking until a rewrite reaches them, provided the manager can vouch for the compile each was minted under: the branch's last accepted compile, or one sent after it that sent the same. The host stores each round as it went out and each reply whole, and defers other writes to the slot until the turn ends (see `acceptRound`). An edit to what came before a reply, made after the walk read it, strips the reply's thinking. A message another namespace's manager stores in a shared slot while a round is out isn't taken as carried, so the reply after it loses its thinking at the next compile.
- For a compile whose thinking is bound (the pass is engaged, and the target binds thinking), `AutobiographicalStrategy` renders and prices the live window as under `carrierPolicy: 'live-strip'`, whatever is configured: a summary's carriers were minted in the summarizer's request, so an enforcing account refuses them there. `'full'` still governs a store whose host has never accepted a round, and a target that doesn't bind thinking. Mint and merge requests are unchanged.
- `acceptRound` syncs the store at every acceptance, so the binding record is durable before the acceptance is reported. That adds a durable flush to each accepted round.
