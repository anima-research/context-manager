- `RenderStats.nesting` — `{ overlapLeaves, renderedTwice, extraTokens }`: the summary-tree nesting invariant (messages listed by more than one L1) and what the compile actually rendered twice. The `[kv-overlap]` warning no longer claims "double-representation in window" — overlap tolerance keeps each exempt leaf on its own lineage, so a non-nested tree can (and on a real store did) render through one summary with nothing duplicated; it now points at `nesting.renderedTwice` and names the planner-stability consequence instead.
