- **Under `foldingStrategy: 'kv-stable'`, the shape prior now measures age in lengths of the attended tail alone, as `docs/kv-stable-context-control.md` describes it.** It used to count the head and every chunk pinned raw as well. So a store with a head window outside the tail, or with pins kept raw outside it, gave some of its other chunks shallower soft fold-depth caps than the same store with those chunks locked raw instead. Such stores can now plan differently wherever the shape prior binds.
