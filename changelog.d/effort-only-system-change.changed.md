- System changes: a `system_change` block carrying only `effort` (`SystemChangeContent.effort`, membrane's release that adds it) changes, from its place on, the effort the model answers with, and membrane sends it at its place, or right after the turn its place falls inside where membrane merges turns. Every strategy keeps it as it keeps any system change: compiled unaltered, in its place among the raw messages, never dropped, and left out of what compression reads. A write refuses a change carrying `effort` beside a change of tools or system text (a non-empty `removeTools`, `defineTools` or `addSystem`), or beside another block in its message, and one whose `effort` isn't `low`, `medium`, `high`, `xhigh` or `max`, each by name; a read leaves such a change out. An effort-only change between a tool call and its result is refused like any other change: kept raw, it would part the call from its result in the same way. The refusal now gives membrane's placement as its reason for a change of tools or system text only: membrane sends one of those after the call's result anyway.
