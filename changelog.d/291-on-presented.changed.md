- `AutobiographicalStrategy` now defines `onPresented` (agent-framework #291), and the manager calls only `onPresented` on a strategy that has it, in legacy mode too. A subclass of `AutobiographicalStrategy` or of `KnowledgeStrategy` that overrides `onNewMessage` to hear each row is no longer called there, and nothing says so: override `onPresented` instead, which is given the rows that entered the window, and call `super.onPresented` so the chunks are rebuilt and compression starts. `onNewMessage` on the class stays, for a caller that drives the strategy itself. A strategy with only `onNewMessage`, such as a `PassthroughStrategy` subclass, is called through it as before.
  - A failed hook of a strategy with `onPresented` is logged as `Strategy onPresented failed for …`, not `Strategy onNewMessage failed for …`: a host that matches that line for an autobiographical or knowledge strategy needs the new prefix. A strategy with only `onNewMessage` keeps the old prefix.
