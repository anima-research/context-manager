- After a head-window reset (`resetHeadWindow`), a compile sends what happened in the order it happened while the new head fills: the history before the reset, then the `[Topic Transition]` message and each message since, so the newest message is the last one sent. Once a message no longer fits `headWindowTokens`, the head goes first, as before: the transition and the new topic's opening, then the history, then the rest. Before, the head went first from the reset on, so while it filled the newest messages came before the old history and the request ended on the old topic.
  - **Whole messages in a reset head.** The transition is always whole in the head, whatever its size. After it, the head takes whole messages (one split into shards as it was stored counts as one) while they fit, and ends before the first that doesn't, or before the one ahead of it when that one holds a tool call, so a call stays with its result. Before, a transition bigger than the window left the hierarchical select's head empty, so the transition itself came after the history, and the adaptive selects ended the head inside a long transition or first message and sent its rest after the history. Without a reset the head still ends where its tokens run out.
  - **Prompt cache.** Before, the reset's compile rewrote the whole request after the system prompt, and each compile in which the head grew rewrote the history again. Now the reset's compile sends the history as before with the transition after it, compiles while the head fills add to the end, and the compile where the head goes first rewrites the request once. A fold of the history while the head fills rewrites the request from that fold on, where before the fill's own rewrite took it in. The usual one is the old head's: the reset makes it compressible, and it sits at the history's front.
  - `renderAsNewest` gives a filling reset head's messages as the compile sends them, last. Before, it gave null for them. Render stats and over-budget refusals still count a filling head as the head. Under the hierarchical select, a compile that can't hold both the history and a filling head refuses at the head, after the history, naming compression when queued work would fold that history.
  - A store whose reset head is filling, or ends inside a message, takes the new order at its first compile after the upgrade, which rewrites its request once.
