- Under `foldingStrategy: 'kv-unified'`, a level pin (`pinAtLevel`, or `pinRange` and `markDocument` with `level`) no longer makes every compile fail while no summary supplies its level over a pinned message (#193). Until compression and merging produce that level, the message is held at the deepest level below it that a summary over it does supply, raw when none does, and it moves to the pinned level once that exists. Compiles that refused with `kv-unified constraints are infeasible …` for such a pin now go through, with the range held finer, and so larger, than pinned until its level exists. Where the budget can't hold the range at the level it's held at, the compile is refused as over budget. A pin placed while its range is in the recent window no longer starts refusing once the range leaves it, and a stored pin whose level no summary supplies any more, such as one whose summary the load repair dropped, is held the same way, where 0.15.0 refused it as infeasible whatever the budget. A compile that finds a pin held logs `autobio: kv-unified holds pin …`, naming the pin, its level and the level its messages are held at, and a later compile that finds it no longer held, or gone, logs that too. The log follows the loaded branch, so after an open or a branch switch a pin that is still held is logged again. A level pin and a lock at another level are still refused, as before, even while the pinned level doesn't exist.
