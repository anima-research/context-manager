- Under `foldingStrategy: 'kv-unified'`, a level pin (`pinAtLevel`, or `pinRange` and `markDocument` with `level`) no longer makes every compile fail while no summary supplies its level over a pinned message (#193). Until compression and merging produce that level, the message is held at the deepest level a summary over it does supply, raw when none does, and it moves to the pinned level once that exists, as under kv-stable. Compiles that refused with `kv-unified constraints are infeasible …` for such a pin now go through, with the range held finer, and so larger, than pinned until its level exists. A pin placed while its range is in the recent window no longer starts refusing once the range leaves it, and a stored pin whose level no summary supplies any more, such as one whose summary the load repair dropped, is held the same way, where 0.15.0 refused it as infeasible whatever the budget. Each hold is logged twice: `autobio: kv-unified holds pin …` when it starts, naming the pin, its level and the level it's held at, and another line when it ends or the pin is removed. A level pin that conflicts with a lock is still refused, as before.
