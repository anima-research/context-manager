- A message removed from the store, or no longer shown by the view's filter, is in no compression or merge request sent from then on (#321). A chunk holding it is rebuilt from the messages it still has before its request, and a compression or merge under way when it leaves sends no further request that carries it, whether to be summarized or as the history before it, such as its ladder's next rung or a retry without its reasoning blocks. Each such discard of a compression writes a `compression:removed-message-discarded` entry to the compression log (`CONTEXT_MANAGER_COMPRESSION_LOG`). The answer to a request sent before it left is kept, as a summary written before it left is. A compile and the combined recall label place a summary at its first message still in the store, and merge grouping by its first and last, so a summary whose first or last message was removed keeps its place, and one that was held out of merges by it is placed again and merges where its neighbours are still unmerged. The repair tooling (`repair-topology`, `strandedInStore`) places such a summary the same way. A summary with no message left stays out of merges, also when the store has no message left at all. `getSummariesInRange()` used to leave out a summary whose first or last message had been removed or hidden; it now lists it, with its span, ids and sequences taken from its first and last messages still in the view, and leaves out only a summary with none left. The live head starts where a restart starts it once its start message is removed, and its cached start follows the store's listing rather than its length. Removal stays non-retroactive: a summary written before a message left keeps its content.
