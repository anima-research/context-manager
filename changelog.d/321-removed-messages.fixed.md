- A message removed from the store, or no longer shown by the view's filter, is in no compression or merge request built after it leaves (#321). A chunk holding it is rebuilt from the messages it still has before its request, and a compression or merge under way when it leaves makes no further call whose request carries it, whether to be summarized or as the history before it, such as its ladder's next rung or a retry without its reasoning blocks. The strategy checks before each call a compression or a merge makes, so a request the transport already holds, such as one membrane sends again after a rate limit, goes out with the messages it was built with. A message the filter stops showing is handled as a removed one. Showing it again afterwards isn't: a summary written while it was hidden leaves it out. When a predicate that changes its answer, which `viewFilter`'s contract forbids, hid the message while its chunk waited, showing it again, by the predicate's answer or by a later open whose filter hides less, while the view still shows that summary's messages on both sides of it, leaves a store that `ContextManager.open` refuses under `topologyPolicy: 'reject'` in legacy mode. Such a store opens with `topologyPolicy: 'report'`, and where no `auxiliaryMessageViews` are merged into its view, `scripts/repair-topology.ts` plans a repair for it. In presented mode, an open under `'reject'` that isn't audit-only drops that summary instead, as it drops any summary the topology audit names, and opens. Each such discard of a compression writes a `compression:removed-message-discarded` entry to the compression log (`CONTEXT_MANAGER_COMPRESSION_LOG`). The answer to a request sent before it left is kept, as a summary written before it left is. A compile and the combined recall label place a summary whose first message was removed at its first message still in the store, and merge grouping by its first and last, so a summary whose first or last message was removed keeps its place, and one that was held out of merges by it is placed again and merges where its neighbours are still unmerged. In a store that already holds such a summary, the first compile after upgrading can place it elsewhere than before, so the request's bytes differ from the first summary that moves, and a prompt cache warmed before the upgrade isn't reused past that point. The repair tooling (`repair-topology`, `strandedInStore`) places such a summary the same way. A summary with no message left, whose range starts and ends at its own messages, stays out of merges, also when the store has no message left at all. `getSummariesInRange()` used to leave out a summary whose first or last message had been removed or hidden; it now lists it, with its span, ids and sequences taken from its first and last messages still in the view, and leaves out only a summary with none left. The live head starts where a restart starts it once its start message is removed, and its cached start follows the store's listing rather than its length. Removal stays non-retroactive: a summary written before a message left keeps its content.
