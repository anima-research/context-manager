- A host can withdraw a compression or merge call it doesn't want made, such as one arriving while it holds the store or stops, by throwing `CompressionWithdrawn`, newly exported, or an error extending it, from the membrane it hands the strategy. Every rung of the L1 compression ladder (the tool-prose hoist, the recall fallbacks, source-only and split-stitch parts) and the merge path rethrow it unchanged and record no outcome, so a withdrawal never counts toward a quarantine. Before, each rung recorded it as a `provider_error`, and a ladder whose remaining rungs failed that way quarantined the chunk. `tick` puts a withdrawn chunk back where an abort leaves it, at the head of its queue, or among the chunks that failed if it failed before, unless a rebuild while the call was out decided the queue afresh, and a withdrawn merge stays at the head of its own. An L1 call whose branch moved while it was out discards the withdrawal, as it discards any error. A withdrawal isn't reported on the console as a failure, the opt-in compression log marks its receipt `withdrawn`, and it stops the speculative drain quietly. The strategy recognises it by a brand that subclasses and other copies of this package carry, not by its name.
