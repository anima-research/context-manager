- A quarantined merge no longer freezes the whole summary pyramid. Merge candidate selection always offered the oldest contiguous run; when that exact group was quarantined, `enqueueMerge` silently declined it on every pass and no later group was ever considered (observed: one refused L2 merge left 322 newer L1s unmerged for 16 days, with zero merge calls). Sources of a quarantined merge are now excluded before contiguity is computed, so they split the frontier like a hole and later history merges around them.
- Merge eligibility is enforced in one place for every scheduler: `enqueueMerge` refuses any group containing a held or quarantined source (previously only an exact quarantined set). The adaptive produce path (`enqueueMergeForRange`, used by kv-stable / kv-unified) also splits its candidates around such sources; before this it re-requested the exact quarantined group on every pick, so the freeze above still occurred on adaptive stores. Persisted queue entries that contain a held or quarantined source are dropped on load.
- Stale merge-quarantine records (sources merged or repaired while the store was closed) are swept at load, not only when the alarm fires.
- An interior run can now consolidate at 2 members even when fewer than `mergeThreshold` summaries are eligible overall (the total-count guard ran before runs were built).
- `getCompressionDebt()` reports `mergeQuarantineKeys` and `mergeHeldIds`.
