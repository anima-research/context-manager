- `mergeHoldSummaryIds`: operator merge holds. Listed summaries are never offered as merge sources by any scheduler and stay on the frontier at their own level (e.g. while awaiting review); like quarantined sources, they split the frontier into runs so later history still merges around them. Holds are deliberate, so they don't change `getCompressionDebt().state`, but they're listed in `mergeHeldIds`. Hosts must pass the option through (connectome-host companion PR).
