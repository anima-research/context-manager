- `switchBranch()` and `fork()` no longer leave the manager initialized for the branch that the store left when the store selects the branch and then fails to save the selection. Chronicle keeps such a selection in effect. The call now rejects, saying that the switch took effect and may not be saved, and that `getStore().sync()` saves it once the store can write. The manager refuses work until `setStrategy(getStrategy())` initializes it on the branch. Where a failed switch leaves the store on another branch, or the store can't say which, the call says so and the manager refuses work too.
