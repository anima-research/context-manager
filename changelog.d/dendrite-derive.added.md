- `ContextManager.derive({ branch, strategy, solve?, atSequence? })` creates a second context manager from this one with shared reads and separate writes (agent-framework #232, "Dendrite"). The derived manager starts from the parent's state at a checkpoint — its messages and everything its strategy persists (summaries with their alternative resolutions, chunks, pins, locks, the carried fold frontier, the kv-unified receipt) — on a Chronicle branch of its own, read through a branch-bound store handle. No log record is copied, the materialized messages are the same objects in memory, and neither manager can alter what the other reads; the parent keeps running and the store's branch cursor is not touched. `getDerivation()` reports the parent branch, own branch, checkpoint and inherited states. Requires a Chronicle build with `JsStore.view` and selective forks (`ContextManager.supportsDerivation(store)`); otherwise `derive` throws `ContextDerivationUnsupportedError`. A derived context persists: `getDerivation()` returns a plain record (branches, checkpoint, inherited states, slot names) and `ContextManager.reopenDerived({ store, derivation, strategy })` reopens the context on its branch after a restart, to resume or to inspect it.
- `solve: 'reuse'` (default) hands the in-memory remainder of the parent strategy's rendering frontier to the child's strategy, so the child's first compile is the compile the parent would have made next, cache markers included. `solve: 'fresh'` discards the inherited frontier on the child's branch and solves from scratch. Strategies opt in through the new `RenderingFrontierStrategy` capability (`exportRenderingFrontier` / `adoptRenderingFrontier` / `discardRenderingFrontier`, with `isRenderingFrontierStrategy`); `AutobiographicalStrategy`, `KnowledgeStrategy` and `WindowedPassthroughStrategy` implement it.
- `MessageStore.deriveOnto(view)` builds a store for the same slot on a branch-bound handle, seeded from the parent's materialized messages instead of re-reading the slot, and sharing the parent's blob resolve cache.
- Strategies can declare which of their persisted states a derived context inherits through the new `DerivationManifestStrategy` capability (`derivationManifest(namespace)`, with `isDerivationManifestStrategy`). Without it, every state under the strategy's namespace prefix is inherited. `AutobiographicalStrategy` (and `KnowledgeStrategy`) inherit summaries, chunk records, the id counter, pins, resolutions, locks, calibration, the kv-unified receipt and the merge quarantine, and leave the merge queue (rebuilt on initialize) and the compression-refusal ledger empty on the child's branch — unless `derive({ inherit: { refusals: true } })` asks for the ledger; `WindowedPassthroughStrategy` inherits its anchor.
