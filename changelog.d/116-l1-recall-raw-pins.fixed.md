A frontier summary that covers a force-raw message is now descended rather than recalled: its children take its place, so only the branch containing the pin opens and the raw added is just the pinned L1 chunks (sibling branches stay summarized). Empty-content frontier summaries are descended the same way instead of exposing their whole span raw. Pin precedence matches the kv-stable selector (`isForceRawPinBound`: a fixed `level` wins over `maxLevel`).
